Return, Refund & Replacement Policy

Your satisfaction matters to us. Learn how ShopSphere handles returns, refunds, and replacements.

Last Updated: October 2026

Our Policy

At ShopSphere, we aim to provide a reliable and convenient shopping experience. If you receive a damaged, defective, incorrect, or otherwise eligible product, you may request a return or replacement according to the applicable product policy.

Please read the following conditions before submitting a return or refund request.

01

Return Policy

Eligible products may be returned within seven days of delivery, subject to the product's return eligibility and the conditions stated in this policy.

Return conditions
  • The product must be eligible for return.
  • The request must be submitted within the applicable return window.
  • The product should be returned with its original packaging, accessories, tags, and invoice where applicable.
  • The product must not have damage caused by misuse, unauthorized modification, or improper handling.
  • ShopSphere may inspect the product before approving the return.
The seven-day return period is a proposed ShopSphere policy. Product-specific terms and applicable consumer protection requirements may differ.
02

Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, contact the ShopSphere support team as soon as reasonably possible after delivery.

Information to provide
  • Your order number.
  • The name of the affected product.
  • A description of the issue.
  • Photographs of the product or damage, where appropriate.

Our team will review the information and explain the available resolution according to the applicable policy.

03

Replacement Policy

A replacement may be available for eligible products that are damaged, defective, incorrect, or otherwise covered by the applicable product policy.

  • Replacement requests are subject to verification.
  • The returned product may be inspected.
  • Replacement depends on product availability.
  • If a suitable replacement is unavailable, our support team will explain the available resolution options.
04

How to Request a Return

Follow these steps to request a return through your ShopSphere account.

Step 1: Log in

Sign in to your ShopSphere customer account.

Step 2: Open My Orders

Find the delivered order containing the product you want to return.

Step 3: Submit a Return Request

Select the eligible product, choose a return reason, and provide the requested details.

Step 4: Track Your Request

Follow the return status and any instructions provided by ShopSphere.

Submitting a request does not automatically approve a return. Return requests must be reviewed according to the applicable policy.
05

Refund Policy

Refunds are processed for eligible orders after the applicable return or cancellation has been approved and any required verification has been completed.

Online payments

Eligible refunds for payments made through Razorpay will generally be issued through the original payment method, subject to payment-provider capabilities and applicable rules.

Cash on Delivery orders

For eligible Cash on Delivery orders, the support team will communicate the available refund method and any information required to process the refund.

Refund processing time

The time required for a refund to reach your account can vary depending on the payment provider, bank, and payment method. A refund being initiated does not necessarily mean the money has already reached your account.

06

Order Cancellation

Orders may be eligible for cancellation before shipment or fulfillment, depending on their current status and the applicable policy.

  • Eligible cancellation requests will be reviewed.
  • Shipped or delivered orders may need to follow the return process instead.
  • Refunds for eligible prepaid cancellations will be handled according to the applicable refund terms.
07

Non-Returnable Products

Some products may be excluded from returns because of their nature, hygiene requirements, customization, applicable legal restrictions, or disclosed product-specific terms.

Any exclusions should be clearly communicated to customers before purchase. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

08

Return Request Status

Once return management is available in ShopSphere, customers will be able to track the progress of their requests through their account.

Request Submitted

Your request has been received.

Return Approved

Your request has been approved.

Product Received

The returned product has been received where a physical return is required.

Refund Completed

The refund has been processed.

Need Help With a Return or Refund?

Our support team can help you understand the applicable return and refund process.
