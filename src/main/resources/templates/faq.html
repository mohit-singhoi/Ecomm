How Can We Help?

ShopSphere is an online shopping platform where customers can browse products from different categories, view product details, add products to their cart, save products for later and place orders.

First, browse our products and select the product you want to purchase. Choose the required quantity and click Add to Cart or Buy Now.

If you add the product to your cart, you can review your cart before proceeding to checkout.

ShopSphere currently offers products in multiple categories including Electronics, Clothes, Stationery, Jewelry and Home Items.

You can visit the Categories page to explore products according to your interests.

Yes. ShopSphere provides a Save for Later option.

You can save a product from your shopping cart and move it back to your cart whenever you are ready to purchase it.

After placing an order, you can view your previous orders from the My Orders section.

The My Orders section displays information such as order details, products, quantity, total amount and order status.

An account allows you to manage your orders, cart, saved items and other shopping activities more conveniently.

You can use the Login option from the navbar to access your account.

Available payment options are displayed during the checkout process.

Select your preferred payment method and complete the required checkout information before placing the order.

You can visit our Contact Us page to get in touch with the ShopSphere support team.

You can also connect with us through WhatsApp for quick assistance.

Your order status can be checked from the My Orders section.

The status may change as your order moves through the order processing process.
