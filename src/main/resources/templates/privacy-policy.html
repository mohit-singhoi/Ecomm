Your privacy matters to us.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ShopSphere. This Privacy Policy explains how ShopSphere collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by users while using our website and services.

By using ShopSphere, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ShopSphere, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping and delivery information
  • Account login information
  • Shopping cart and wishlist information
  • Order and transaction-related information

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through ShopSphere may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Processing and managing orders
  • Providing customer support

When you create a ShopSphere account, you are responsible for providing accurate information and keeping your account credentials secure.

You should not share your account password with another person.

Payment processing may be handled through a third-party payment provider. ShopSphere does not intend to store complete payment card details on its own servers.

Payment information may be processed according to the privacy and security policies of the applicable payment provider.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ShopSphere may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functionality, maintain sessions, and improve the user experience.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through ShopSphere. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

ShopSphere may use third-party services for functions such as payment processing, hosting, analytics, email delivery, or other technical services.

Such services may process information according to their own applicable policies.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain account and order records, resolve disputes, comply with applicable requirements, or fulfill legitimate business purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

ShopSphere is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the functionality available on ShopSphere, you may be able to review or update certain account information through your account.

You may also contact ShopSphere regarding questions about your personal information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the ShopSphere Contact Us page.
